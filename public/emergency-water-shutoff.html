Where Is Your Main Valve?

Top 4 Places to Find Your Main Water Shutoff in Lynnwood

Homes built in Lynnwood, Alderwood Manor, and Martha Lake typically have their main line enter at one of these locations:

1

Basement / Crawlspace Entry

Look on the foundation wall facing the street where the main water pipe enters through the concrete. Look for a brass lever or circular round handle.

2

Garage / Utility Closet

In slab-on-grade homes common in newer Lynnwood developments, the main valve is often located near the water heater closet or along the perimeter garage wall.

3

Curb Meter Box (Street Side)

At the front property edge near the sidewalk or street curb inside a metal or plastic lid marked "WATER". Requires a T-shaped water meter curb stop key.

4

Near Pressure Regulator (PRV)

Mounted right next to the bell-shaped brass pressure reducing valve that regulates Lynnwood city water pressure before distributing indoors.
