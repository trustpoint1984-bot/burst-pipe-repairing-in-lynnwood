Early Warning Signs

5 Symptoms of a Foundation Slab Leak

Underground pressurized leaks wash away structural soil beneath your concrete footings, leading to foundation shifts if left unaddressed.

🔥

Warm Floor Spots

Unusually warm hardwood or vinyl flooring caused by hot water line breaches.

🌊

Sound of Running Water

A continuous hissing or rushing sound even when all faucets and toilets are shut off.

📈

Skyrocketing Utility Bills

Unexplained surges in your Lynnwood or Alderwood Water District monthly bill.

💧

Damp Baseboards

Moisture wicking up drywall edges or musty mildew smells in low-lying rooms.

⚠️

Floor Cracking

Cracks developing along tile grout lines or visible slab heaving.
